If I had to describe myself in three words, they would be adaptable, creative, and structured. I feel comfortable navigating new environments and unfamiliar situations while remaining grounded and approachable; something my friends often describe as being down-to-earth. My way of working is best described as structured-creative: I use visualization tools and clear systems to organize ideas, make connections, and turn early concepts into practical outcomes.

I was born and raised in Peru, where I began my higher education before deciding to pursue a different path. After my first year, I moved to the Netherlands, completed a BSc in International Business Administration, and later pursued an MSc in Management of Innovation. Moving abroad challenged me to adapt, build a life in a new country, and become more independent.

Throughout my studies, I actively looked for opportunities to apply what I was learning beyond the classroom. This led me to consulting projects, internships, hackathons, and international team experiences, several of which are featured throughout this portfolio. 

Outside work, I enjoy hiking, travelling, cooking, photography, caring for plants, and maintaining an active lifestyle. Exploring different countries and cultures continues to shape how I see the world and work with others. Having completed the Tour du Mont Blanc, I hope to take on Kilimanjaro in the coming years.
