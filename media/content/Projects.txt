AkzoNobel Innovation Clinic
(Building)

Oct 2025 – Jun 2026

Working in a team of Master’s students from Rotterdam School of Management, we explored how AkzoNobel could better understand employee experiences, concerns, and attitudes during technological change. Through employee interviews and organisational research, we found that collecting feedback is not enough on its own: employees also need clarity about who receives their input, who is responsible for acting on it, and how outcomes are communicated back.

Based on these findings, we developed a structured feedback concept built around three principles: accessible feedback, transparent communication loops, and psychological safety. Drawing on Kaizen-inspired methodologies, the concept emphasised continuous, incremental improvement through recurring employee input, clear ownership, and visible follow-up. The recommendation aimed to make feedback easy to provide, locally relevant, responsibly managed, and visibly acknowledged, without creating another burdensome internal process.

We concluded the project by presenting our research, proposed framework, and implementation considerations at AkzoNobel’s head office in Amsterdam. The experience strengthened my ability to translate qualitative employee insights into a practical organisational solution and reinforced the importance of trust, communication, continuous improvement, and human-centred change management in digital transformation.

SDG Challenge (IO Digital) - Best Pitch Award
(Trophy)

Feb 2026 – May 2026

Won Best Pitch at the SDG Challenge for BAMBI, a Slack-based microlearning concept developed for iO Digital to strengthen cross-departmental awareness and collaboration.

Working in a multidisciplinary team, we investigated a behavioural challenge within cross-functional collaboration: employees may have access to internal knowledge and communication tools, but this does not necessarily help them understand how other departments think, prioritise, and make decisions.

BAMBI addresses this challenge through short, role-based workplace scenarios delivered through Slack. Employees are encouraged to consider situations from another department’s perspective and receive feedback explaining the trade-offs behind different decisions. The concept combines microlearning, behavioural nudges, perspective-taking, and repeated engagement to make cross-functional learning more accessible and habitual.

The project was informed by academic literature, stakeholder consultation, behavioural frameworks such as EAST, and case studies on workplace learning and employee engagement. Our final recommendation positioned BAMBI not as another internal platform, but as a lightweight engagement layer that complements iO Digital’s existing infrastructure and makes collaboration easier to initiate and repeat.

The project strengthened my experience in translating behavioural and organisational research into a feasible workplace innovation and communicating its strategic value through an award-winning pitch.

Samsung Solve for Tomorrow - Finalist
(Samsung)
Aug 2025 – Nov 2025

Reached the finals during the Samsung Solve for Tomorrow, a student innovation initiative combining technology, sustainability, and social impact.

Working  in a multidisciplinary team, we developed a circular logistics packaging concept aimed at reducing waste from single-use plastic wrapping and conventional pallets. Our proposed solution combined reusable protective wrapping with compatible pallets made from recycled or sustainable materials, designed to be durable, easy to install, removable, and suitable for repeated use.

We followed an iterative development process, refining the concept through material assessment, mentor feedback, and prioritized the development of a clear business value proposition.

Our project reached the finals at the Samsung Benelux offices at Schiphol. The experience reinforced that meaningful innovation requires more than a strong idea: it depends on direct engagement with experts and users, continuous testing, effective communication, and the ability to balance creativity, feasibility, and social responsibility.
